v— AeroDoc

The access registry,
in your hands.

A separate build of AeroDoc that manages who may run it: approve and revoke accounts, grant access for a period you set, free a device slot, and switch device binding off for one person or everyone.

This page is open on purpose. The admin build is only a screen. Authority lives in the Firestore security rules, not in the binary: anyone who downloads it can sign in and will be refused every read and every write unless their account is an administrator. There is nothing here worth hiding, and hiding it would protect nothing.

Approve & revoke

New accounts arrive pending and stay locked out until you approve them. Revoking says so plainly on the user's screen — it never reverts to “waiting for approval”.

Access for a period

Grant access until a date you choose, then extend it or clear it. The end date travels to the device, so going offline cannot outrun it.

Device slots

One Windows and one Android device per account, each free to move once every 30 days. Unbind a slot to override that instantly when a phone breaks or a laptop is reimaged.

REQUIREMENTS

Windows 10 or later · 64-bit
Android 7.0 or later
Account an administrator account · a connection at all times
